(Q.No. 3 to 6)

Note : Attempt all questions. Answer each question in 75 words. Each question carries 10 marks.

3.Explain the offences of voluntarily causing hurt for the purpose of extorting property, of extorting a confession, and of deterring a public servant from the discharge of his duty, under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023. State the punishment provided in each case.[10]
4.Explain the offence of kidnapping or abducting a person in order that such person may be murdered, and the offence of kidnapping or abducting for ransom, under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023. Support your answer giving illustrations.[10]
5.“Culpable homicide is not murder if the offender, whilst deprived of the power of self-control by grave and sudden provocation, causes the death of the person who gave the provocation or causes the death of any other person by mistake or accident.”
Explain the above exception to section 300 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860, and state the provisos to which it is subject. Discuss with the help of decisions of the Supreme Court.[10]
6.Explain the offences under sections 336, 337 and 338 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860 relating to acts done so rashly or negligently as to endanger human life or the personal safety of others. Point out the difference between them.[10]

(Q.No. 7 to 10)

Note : Attempt all questions. Answer each question in 150 words. Each question carries 20 marks.

7.“Whoever causes death by doing an act with the intention of causing death, or with the intention of causing such bodily injury as is likely to cause death, or with the knowledge that he is likely by such act to cause death, commits the offence of culpable homicide.”
Comment on the above statement and distinguish culpable homicide from murder under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023. State the punishment provided for murder and for culpable homicide not amounting to murder. Discuss with the help of decisions of the Supreme Court.[20]
8.“Whoever voluntarily causes hurt, if the hurt which he intends to cause or knows himself to be likely to cause is grievous hurt, and if the hurt which he causes is grievous hurt, is said voluntarily to cause grievous hurt.”
Comment on the above statement and explain the aggravated forms of this offence under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, including the punishment where the hurt causes permanent disability or a persistent vegetative state, and where grievous hurt is caused by a group of five or more persons acting in concert on the ground of race, caste or community.[20]
9.“Whoever wrongfully restrains any person in such a manner as to prevent that person from proceeding beyond certain circumscribing limits, is said wrongfully to confine that person.”
Comment on the above statement and explain the aggravated forms of wrongful confinement under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, with reference to the period of confinement, confinement in secret, and confinement for the purpose of extorting property or a confession. Support your answer giving illustrations.[20]
10.“Whoever voluntarily causes a woman with child to miscarry, shall, if such miscarriage be not caused in good faith for the purpose of saving the life of the woman, be punished.”
Comment on the above statement with reference to section 312 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860. Distinguish it from causing miscarriage without the consent of the woman and from death caused by an act done with intent to cause miscarriage. Whether a woman who causes herself to miscarry is within the section? Explain it.[20]
